[13th World Congress of the Bachelier Finance Society](https://www.bacheliercongress.com/2026/eventi.unibo.it/bachelier.html), July 2026, Bologna, Italy. 
Cialenco and Ludkovski organize a thematic session "Stochastic Games in Environmental Finance", featuring 


1. Comparison of Tax and Cap-and-Trade Carbon Pricing Schemes, by Stéphane Crépey (Universite Paris-Cite, 🇫🇷)

2. Cooperation, Competition, and Common Pool Resources in Mean Field Games and extensions with Learning by Gokce Dayanikli (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, 🇺🇸)

3. Dynamic Multi-Period Groundwater Markets by Mike Ludkovski
(University of California, Santa Barbara, 🇺🇸)

4. Pro-Rata Market Design for Natural Resource Allocation by 
Igor Cialenco (Illinois Institute of Technology, 🇺🇸)
