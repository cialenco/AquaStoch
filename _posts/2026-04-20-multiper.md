Following up on our [earlier post](_posts/2025-01-23-sifin.md) we discuss multi-period groundwater markets. 

### The Model

We model the interaction as a non-cooperative game among $$J$$ economic agents who each grows several crops. 
The key ingredient in the dynamic model is the opportunity for individual stakeholders to bank their pumping rights. Banking reduces available water today and increases groundwater available next period, reducing \eqref{eq:dynWj} to 
$$
W_j(t+1) = b_j(t) + R_j(t+1)  \quad t=0,\ldots, T-1.
$$
The assumption that $b_j(t)$ is non-negative is essential and implies that agents cannot borrow from their future allocations, but must first build up pumping reserves.
In turn, in the present period, available water is composed of consumption, trading and banking:
$$
W_j(t) =  C_j(t) - \psi_j(t) + b_j(t)
$$
with the market clearing condition $\sum_{j=1}^J \psi_j(t) = 0$. Each agent $j=1,\ldots,J$ selects a control $\pi_j(t) = \big(C_j(t), \psi_j(t)\big)$ at each period $t$ from the feasible action set
$$
\cal A_j(\mathbf{W}):= \mathcal{C}_j(t) \times [-\sum_{i\neq j} W_j(t), {W}_j(t)].
$$ 

The state process is $\{\mathbf{W}(t), R(t)\}$ which tracks the groundwater rights vector as well as the recharge regime that is assumed to follow a Markov chain.
Each agent $j$  
maximizes her expected risk-adjusted payoff 
\[
A_j(\pi_j, \pi_{-j},p) = \mathbb{E}^{\pi} \Big[\sum_{s=0}^T U_j(L_j(s; \pi_j(s),p(s)))\Big], \qquad L_j(t) := G_j(t,C_j(t)) + \psi_j(t) \cdot p(t).  
\]
where $U_j$ is a concave utility function. 


### Sub-Game Perfect Markov Nash Equilibrium 

We consider *only  Markovian policies* $\mathbf{\pi}, p$, on the state space $\cal S$. Thus, the process $(\boldsymbol{W}(t), \mathbf{R}(t))$  is Markov. 
In equilibrium, the value of the continuation game of the agent $j$,  
$$
A_j(\pi_j, \mathbf{\pi}_{-j}, p; t, \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{r}) = \mathbb{E}^{\pi} \Big[\sum_{s=t}^T U_j\big( L_j(s)\big) \ \big| \  \boldsymbol{W}(t)= \mathbf{w}, \ \mathbf{R}(t)= \mathbf{r} \Big], \quad j=1,\ldots,J,
$$
is maximized for each $j$.  Denote the  best-response continuation  value as 
$$
\cal V_j( \pi_{-j}, p; t, \mathbf{w}, r) = \sup_{\pi\in\mathscr{S}_j}  A_j(\pi, \mathbf{\pi}_{-j}, p; t, \mathbf{w}, r). 
$$
Then the game value for sub-game perfect NE can be recursively constructed by
$$
\cal V_j(\mathbf{\pi}_{-j}, p; t, \mathbf{w}, r) & = \max_{C_j, \psi_j}\Big[U_j \bigl( G_j(t, C_j) + \psi_j p \bigr) \\
 + \mathbb{E}^{\mathbf{\pi}}[ \cV_j(\mathbf{\pi}_{-j}^\circ, p^\circ; t+1, \mathbf{w} + \mathbf{R}(t+1) - \mathbf{C} - \boldsymbol{\psi}, R(t+1) ) \mid \\mathbf{W}(t) = \mathbf{w}, R(t) = r] \Big]
$$
